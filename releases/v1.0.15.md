# AoE3 Mod Launcher v1.0.15

This release brings a new rating system with placement matches, streaks and a team ladder
where you pick your side in the room. It also redesigns the Rooms page and the Ranking, makes
Repair much more careful, and lets you follow translations from any translator.

## New rating system
- **Placement matches.** Your first 10 rated matches in 1v1 (5 in Teams) place you. Until then
  your rating shows with a "?" (for example "1580?") and you appear at the bottom of the table
  as "Placement 6/10".
- **The table is ordered by rating**, highest first, so the ELO column and the bars always go
  down together.
- **Your rating never decays.** After 30 days without a rated match you get an INACTIVE tag and
  keep your place. After a long break, your first matches move your rating more.
- **Streaks.** From 3 wins in a row a 🔥 badge with the number appears. Your profile keeps your
  longest winning and losing streaks.
- **Beating the same opponent again and again counts less.** From the 3rd win in a row over the
  same player the points drop, down to 20 %. They recover when the other player wins, and a
  little more each day apart. The result card and History show it.
- **New accounts:** a short match between a very new account and someone on the same network
  does not count.
- **Refunds.** When a cheater is banned, the points you lost to them come back. You get one
  notification and a banner on your Profile.
- **Every past rated match was recalculated under the new rules.** Your rating will change, and
  many players start in placement again.

## Team games
- **Pick your team in the room.** In 4- and 6-seat rooms every player picks team 1 or 2, and
  the host can move anyone. Start waits until every seat is taken, everyone has a team and the
  sides are even; the button says what is missing. **Everyone in the room needs this version.**
- **The sides in the game must match the room's.** If they don't, the match doesn't count and
  the result card names the sides the game had.
- **Win chance.** Competitive rooms show each side's chance of winning.
- **Fixed: the first competitive 2v2s scored nothing.** The launcher now reads the sides and
  every resignation from the recording, and keeps sending each player's in-game name until the
  server has it.
- **Team badge.** Your team rank is two shields. In your Profile you choose which badge others
  see outside a room: the highest, 1v1 or Teams. Inside a room, the badge always matches the
  room.
- **The rank guide has 1v1 and Teams tabs**, and "How ranks work" is now in your account menu.

## After a match
- **A new result card** shows your streak, why a win was worth less, why a match didn't count,
  or "You enter the table at rank 7" when you finish placement.
- **Clearer reasons when a match doesn't count**, for example "Casual room" or "Record Game was
  off".
- **History:** each card shows mode · map · start time. Tournament games carry a tag, team games
  name both sides, and the days read TODAY and YESTERDAY.

## Ranking
- **The table and "Latest matches" share the page** at any window size, and the table grows with
  the window.
- **Every player has their Discord picture**, and the columns are ELO, W-L and %.
- **New "Highlights" view:** seven highlights of the month (biggest climb, most wins, most
  matches, best streak, best win rate, biggest upset, civilization of the month), each with its
  top five.
- **On the 1st of each month**, last month's highlights are posted on Discord.
- **Fixed: a cut-off match line** could show a flag after the "…" with no name. Hovering it now
  shows the whole line, flags included.

## Rooms page
- **One "Community activity" block at the bottom:** the month's highlights, matches and players,
  the most played map, the top five, peak hours and the latest matches. Hide/Show is remembered,
  and a link shows last month.
- **The room list comes first.** It always keeps room for at least 4 rooms and scrolls on its
  own. With no rooms, it shows the busiest hours and a "+ Create room" button.
- **The chat is a fixed column** that folds to a narrow rail with an unread count.
- **Paste a room code or a join link into the search box** to join, private rooms included. The
  separate code field is gone.
- **"Help connecting" moved** into the "Connected ▾" menu at the top, next to your Radmin IP.
- **Room rows:** a CASUAL tag, one bar per seat, and a coloured ping.
- **Even spacing** on every Multiplayer page, and **lower header bars on laptop-sized windows**
  (the same content, just less tall).
- **Fixed: your search was cleared** every time the room list refreshed with a change.
- **Fixed (a player's request): inviting someone who is in a match** said "Invite sent" while
  the invite was lost. Now the invite button tells you they are playing, and nothing is sent.

## Profile
- **One card per mode:** peak and lowest rating, streaks, placement progress, and your record
  against each opponent.
- **New "Matches" section:** your recorded games against people and your games against the AI,
  which now name the AI ("Lost · vs Ali Pasha").
- **Decks:** flags on the deck buttons, the game's own number in each card's corner, and the
  age a card can be sent in. Decks from other mods are hidden.
- **Fixed (reported): dates and times followed the Windows language** instead of the launcher's.
- **Fixed: after switching mods, the Profile kept showing the first mod's decks.**

## Tournaments
- **Fixed: organisers could not start a tournament from the launcher** — "Seed" and "Draw the
  bracket" were out of reach. Reopening registration is now in the ⋯ menu.
- **Fixed: "Make co-organiser" appeared on the owner's own row.**
- **Team matches remind you which side to play** in the match's action bar.

## Repair and Verify
- **Verify and Repair now give the same answer.**
- **A file another program has open is not "damaged".** You are told to close that program, and
  nothing is downloaded. Files blocked by your antivirus get their own message.
- **When Verify finds problems**, a card offers **Repair** and **Copy list** (to paste on
  Discord).
- **Damaged game files are restored from your own Age of Empires III** when it has an identical
  copy, with no download.
- **Leftover base-game files are moved aside with no download.** This fixes the installs that
  v1.0.14s said needed a reinstall — the game in your AoE3's language or with vanilla data:
  press Repair.
- **Repair asks before a big download** and says how big it is. That question and the low-space
  warning now match the launcher's dark look.
- **The admin prompt comes last**, right before anything is written, and the launcher carries on
  by itself after you accept.
- **A repair or update that didn't finish** is flagged the next time you open the launcher.
- **Fixed in Repair:**
  - it could switch the mod to another version (a pinned version, or a downgrade of Improvement
    Mod);
  - it put the game back in English while the launcher still showed your translation;
  - after it, disabling an addon could restore nothing;
  - repairing a second copy moved the main copy's desktop shortcut and Windows entry to it;
  - copies of Napoleonic Era or Struggle of Indonesia could ask for the product key;
  - its result was replaced by "Up to date" after a few seconds, and a failure only said
    "Operation failed" — now the reason is shown, with a Retry button;
  - it was offered for mods the launcher does not install; it is now disabled, with the reason.
- **Fixed in downloads:** Pause did nothing, Cancel could leave half-written files, and a corrupt
  download during a repair now offers to download again.
- **"Share diagnostics" is easier to find:** it is step 2 of "Something not working?" and has
  its own box in the side panel.
- **The search in the mod window** shows a list of results and finds things in English or
  Spanish, whatever the launcher's language.

## Translations
- **Follow other translators.** At the bottom of a mod's Language tab, "Translation sources"
  takes an `owner/repo`, a GitHub link, or a link to a `translations-index.json` on Google
  Drive, Dropbox, a gist or a website. A link that cannot work is refused with the reason.
- **A translator can share a one-click link** that adds their source, after you confirm.
- **One card per translator**, with a version picker, and "Update" on the card in use when its
  translator publishes something newer.
- **New versions appear on their own.** The notification names the translator and opens the
  Language tab.
- **A warning when the game isn't actually using your translation**, with a way to fix it.
- **Fixed (reported): a translation could stay marked "In use"** when it wasn't applied, and
  switching back to English failed with "Could not revert to English".
- **Downloads are checked against the hash the translator published.**
- **For translators:** the packager proposes versions like `1.2.0e-r1` and can write your
  `translations-index.json` for you.

## Other
- **Fixed (reported): Age of Empires III was not detected** in folders like "Age of Empires
  III - Complete Collection".
- **Fixed (reported): choosing the AoE3 folder by hand** only took effect after restarting the
  launcher.
- **Games against the AI** in a mod's Statistics now say who you played.
- **Secondary buttons** in Settings and the mod window have a soft fill instead of an empty
  outline.

# AoE3 Mod Launcher v1.0.15 (Español)

Esta versión trae un sistema de clasificación nuevo, con partidas de posicionamiento, rachas y
una clasificación por equipos donde eliges tu bando en la sala. También rediseña la página de
Salas y la Clasificación, hace que Reparar sea mucho más cuidadoso y te deja seguir traducciones

## Sistema de clasificación nuevo
- **Partidas de posicionamiento.** Tus primeras 10 partidas puntuadas en 1v1 (5 en Equipos) te
  posicionan. Hasta entonces tu ELO se muestra con un «?» (por ejemplo «1580?») y apareces al
  final de la tabla como «Posicionamiento 6/10».
- **La tabla se ordena por ELO**, de mayor a menor, así la columna de ELO y las barras siempre
  bajan juntas.
- **Tu ELO nunca baja por no jugar.** Tras 30 días sin una partida puntuada llevas la etiqueta
  INACTIVO y conservas tu puesto. Después de un descanso largo, tus primeras partidas mueven más
  tu ELO.
- **Rachas.** Desde 3 victorias seguidas aparece una insignia 🔥 con el número. Tu perfil guarda
  tus rachas más largas de victorias y de derrotas.
- **Ganarle una y otra vez al mismo rival cuenta menos.** Desde la 3.ª victoria seguida contra
  el mismo jugador los puntos bajan, hasta un 20 %. Se recuperan cuando gana el otro, y un poco
  más con cada día sin jugar entre ustedes. Lo muestran la tarjeta del resultado y el Historial.
- **Cuentas nuevas:** una partida corta entre una cuenta muy nueva y alguien de la misma red no
  cuenta.
- **Devoluciones.** Cuando se banea a un tramposo, recuperas los puntos que perdiste contra él.
  Recibes un aviso y un cartel en tu Perfil.
- **Todas las partidas puntuadas anteriores se recalcularon con las reglas nuevas.** Tu ELO va a
  cambiar, y muchos jugadores empiezan de nuevo en posicionamiento.

## Partidas por equipos
- **Elige tu equipo en la sala.** En salas de 4 y 6 plazas cada jugador elige el equipo 1 o 2, y
  el anfitrión puede mover a cualquiera. Iniciar espera a que estén todas las plazas ocupadas,
  todos tengan equipo y los bandos estén parejos; el botón dice qué falta. **Todos en la sala
  necesitan esta versión.**
- **Los bandos dentro del juego tienen que coincidir con los de la sala.** Si no coinciden, la
  partida no cuenta y la tarjeta del resultado dice qué bandos tuvo el juego.
- **Probabilidad de victoria.** Las salas competitivas muestran la probabilidad de ganar de cada
  bando.
- **Arreglado: las primeras 2v2 competitivas no puntuaron.** El launcher ahora lee los bandos y
  cada rendición de la grabación, y sigue enviando el nombre de cada jugador en el juego hasta
  que el servidor lo tiene.
- **Insignia de equipos.** Tu rango por equipos son dos escudos. En tu Perfil eliges qué
  insignia ven los demás fuera de una sala: la más alta, 1v1 o Equipos. Dentro de una sala, la
  insignia siempre coincide con la sala.
- **La guía de rangos tiene pestañas 1v1 y Equipos**, y «Cómo funcionan los rangos» ahora está
  en el menú de tu cuenta.

  una partida no contó, o «Entras en la tabla en el puesto 7» al terminar el posicionamiento.
