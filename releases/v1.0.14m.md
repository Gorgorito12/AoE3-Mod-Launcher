One fix, and it only concerns you if you have **Knights and Barbarians** installed — but if you do,
you want this build. Everything in
[1.0.14l](https://github.com/Gorgorito12/AoE3-Mod-Launcher/blob/main/releases/v1.0.14l.md) is
unchanged.

## Fixes

- **Knights and Barbarians is recognised again after its rename.** The mod is now published as
  *Knights and Barbarians-Remastered*, and until this build a renamed mod was simply a different
  mod: the old entry vanished from the catalog, the new one looked like something you had never
  installed, and the folder already on your disk read as belonging to somebody else's mod — so the
  launcher offered you a fresh multi-gigabyte download for a copy sitting right there. Opening this
  version carries your install path, your mod collection, your favourites and your update history
  over to the new name, and the folder is recognised as yours again.

  **Nothing is downloaded, and nothing on your disk is moved or renamed.** Your saved games in
  `Documents\My Games\Knights and Barbarians` are untouched, and the entry in Add or remove
  programs stays the one you already had. If you never installed the mod there is nothing to carry
  over, and nothing changes for you.

  It is not a patch for one mod either: from here on a mod can be renamed in the catalog without
  costing anybody the copy they already have.

## Arreglos

- **Knights and Barbarians vuelve a reconocerse tras cambiar de nombre.** El mod ahora se publica
  como *Knights and Barbarians-Remastered*, y hasta esta versión un mod renombrado era sencillamente

  actualizaciones pasan al nombre nuevo, y la carpeta vuelve a reconocerse como tuya.

  **No se descarga nada, y nada de tu disco se mueve ni se renombra.** Tus partidas guardadas en
  `Documentos\My Games\Knights and Barbarians` quedan intactas, y la entrada de Agregar o quitar
  programas sigue siendo la que ya tenías. Si nunca instalaste el mod no hay nada que migrar, y para
  ti no cambia nada.

  Tampoco es un parche para un mod concreto: a partir de ahora se puede renombrar un mod en el
  catálogo sin que nadie pierda la copia que ya tiene.
