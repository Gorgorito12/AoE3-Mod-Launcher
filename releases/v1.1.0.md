The biggest release so far: a new Library, a quick match that finds you a rival, and almost every
multiplayer screen redrawn.

## A new Library
- **The Library and the Workshop are one tab.** Every mod of the catalog is in the same list as
  the ones you have, and one button installs, updates or plays the mod you picked.
- **The list has groups** (Favorites, My mods with the most played first, Catalog), a search box,
  filters and sorting. Each row says where the mod stands: update available, a download stopped at
  42 %, how many copies you have.
- **The selected mod's cover fills the window**, behind glass panels, and the window takes that
  mod's colour. A mod that declares no colour gets one from its icon.
- **Each mod's page** has a screenshot gallery (GIFs play), its description with "Read more", and
  chips that say how it installs and how it updates.
- **A Downloads card** shows what is downloading, with Pause and Resume.
- **Right-click a mod** for Play, Check for updates, Open folder, Remove from my mods or Uninstall.
- **"I have it: choose its folder"** uses a copy you already have instead of downloading it again,
  and a picker chooses which installed copy opens.
- **The list can be folded to icons**, and folds by itself in a narrow window.
- **Switching between mods no longer waits for the network.**

## Quick match
- **A "Quick match" bar on top of Rooms.** Pick the mod and Rated, Casual or Either, press search,
  and the launcher finds you a 1v1.
- **"Rival found!"** shows you and your rival face to face, the points at stake, and 20 seconds to
  accept, with the mod's image behind. Whoever accepts turns green. It comes forward by itself,
  with its own sound, even with the launcher in the tray.
- **Players who are searching appear in the rooms list** with a "Play" button to challenge them.
- **Declining or letting the clock run out counts as a strike.** The first is only a warning; a few
  in a row pause your searches for some minutes.
- **The search follows you**: a timer in the title bar on every tab, and "Find another" on the
  result of a rated 1v1.
- **Optional notice when somebody is looking for a 1v1** (Settings, off by default).

## Rooms
- **The rooms list is redrawn.** Rooms are grouped by whether you can enter, are being played or
  are unavailable, and each row has one button that says what you can do: Join, See match, Install
  mod, or Check my copy when your version is different.
- **The ping column is your ping to each host** over Radmin, with bars in green, amber or red.
  Your own connection is shown once, above the list.
- **A new room shows up in about a second.**
- **Game rules when you create a room**: Standard, Classic, treaties, King of the Hill. Rated rooms
  always play Standard. Everyone sees the rules of a room before entering.
- **Click a player's name or photo almost anywhere to open their profile.**
- **You can watch a match in progress** from its row: who is playing, how long it has run, and the
  result when it ends.

## The room window
- **A new room window**, with one set of buttons and a line that says what the room is waiting for.
- **In a rated room, a card shows what is at stake**: the points you win or lose, and your chance.
- **Each player's ping under their name.**
- **The host marks ready like everybody else.** A full room with everyone ready starts its
  countdown by itself; in a casual room the host can also "Start without waiting".
- **Rematch**: opens the same room again and invites your rival.
- **The result explains itself**: "+21: you won without being the favourite", or why a match did
  not count.

## The game can enter the LAN room by itself
- **No more walking through the menus.** With a small helper, the game hosts or joins the room's
  LAN game on its own: the host creates it, everyone else connects to the host.
- **The launcher asks first**, once, the first time you enter a room, and says exactly which two
  files it adds to the game's folder. It works with Wars of Liberty and the base game.
- **A switch in the room turns it off for a match**, and Settings › Games removes it for good.
- **It is new.** If your game stays on the menu, enter by hand as always and tell us on Discord.

## Tournaments
- **A new tournaments page**: what is yours to do on top, the bracket with zoom and drag, the
  participants and the organiser's tools in tabs.
- **One window to enter**, alone or with a team, that is also where you see or withdraw your entry.
- **Private tournaments**: only people with the code can see and enter them.
- **Organisers can watch a match and decide it** from the same window when it has to be settled by
  hand.
- **"My teams" is redone**: create a team, invite who is online or by name, answer an invitation
  from the notice itself.
- **Finished tournaments stay** in the list for good.

## Ranking, profiles and statistics
- **A podium for the top three** and a "Your position" card: who is just above you and how many
  points you need to pass them.
- **Highlights as a mosaic**: who climbed the most with where they started and where they got to,
  bars, streaks counted win by win, the biggest upset as a duel, and the civilization of the month.
- **Other players' profiles**, with your record against them. Yours adds streaks, your rating's
  curve and your civilizations.
- **"Show my ELO"** in Settings: turn it off and others see "Hidden" instead of your number.
- **A player still being placed shows "In placement 3/10"** to others, not a number.
- **30 days without a rated match takes you off the table.** You keep your ELO and come back with
  your next rated match.
- **Statistics fill the page**, without empty columns.

## Mods: install, update and repair
- **"Update mods automatically" now does it**, in the background, never while you play or sit in a
  room. The download limit in Settings now limits downloads too.
- **Repair downloads only the files that are damaged** when it can, instead of the whole mod.
- **Every download from GitHub is checked** against the hash GitHub publishes for it.
- **The install window stops you when the disk is short**, instead of warning and going on.
- **Updating the launcher**: its window says what it is doing, the notes open in your language,
  and the gold button in the title bar becomes "Restart to update" once the download is checked.
  An open launcher also looks for a new version every half hour.

## Settings, notifications and uninstalling
- **Appearance**: how transparent the panels are, the mod's image in the background, each mod's
  colour, and effects (Reduced makes everything solid and still).
- **"Do not disturb"** silences pop-ups about news. Invitations to a room, your tournament match
  and answers to what you just did still show. The bell keeps everything.
- **A new notification bell**: filters, grouped by day, and buttons on each notice.
- **Smaller, clearer pop-ups**, and every question the launcher asks is now in its own style.
- **Mod settings are redone**: your copies in one card, Verify in General, and a search box.
- **"Uninstall from this PC"** is a real window: it lists what it removes, names the mods it does
  not touch, and keeps your data unless you tick the box. A portable .exe gets "Remove from this
  PC".
- **The Radmin assistant has three steps** and can install Radmin VPN for you after checking the
  installer is its maker's; if that fails it opens the download page.

## Fixes
- **The launcher no longer uses more and more memory** the longer it stays open.
- **Going back to Statistics, Ranking or Matches no longer lags.**
- **A minimised launcher no longer keeps asking the server** for rooms and matches.
- **The "opened a room" card in the chat follows its room**: it used to offer Join for ever, even
  for a room that was full or gone.
- **Ranking and Tournaments say when they could not load**, instead of "nobody is ranked yet".
- **The order you chose for the tabs is respected.**
- **Your settings are no longer lost** when the launcher saved them from two places at once.
- **The launcher warns you before entering a room when your session is about to expire**, so a
  match's result is not lost on the way.
